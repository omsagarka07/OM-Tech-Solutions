Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

How long does it take to build a website?
Most projects are delivered within 3–7 days depending on complexity. Starter websites usually take 3 days, business websites 5–7 days, and premium web apps may take 2–4 weeks.
Do you provide hosting and domain?
Yes! We can help you set up hosting and domain. Basic hosting is available free on GitHub Pages, or we can set up custom domain hosting for your business.
Will my website be mobile-friendly?
Absolutely! Every website we build is 100% responsive and tested on mobile, tablet and desktop devices to ensure perfect display everywhere.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and bank transfer. Usually 50% advance and 50% after delivery. Flexible for small projects.
Do you provide support after delivery?
Yes! We provide free support for 15 days after delivery for any bugs or issues. After that, we offer affordable maintenance packages.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Definitely! We can redesign, modernize or add new features to your existing website. Just share the URL and requirements — we'll suggest the best approach.
